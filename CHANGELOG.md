# Changelog

What changed in each version of Afterburner Profile Backup. Newest first.

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-18)

### Fixed
- The window no longer looks squashed when Windows display zoom is above 100% (for example 125% or 150%). The window now grows with your zoom setting.
- On small screens, every screen can now scroll, so no button is ever cut off. Pressing Tab scrolls to the button you move to.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

First release.

- **Back Up My Profiles** — copies your profiles into a new dated folder (any drive, or your NAS) and checks every copy.
- **Restore Profiles** — pick a backup, tick the profiles you want, and put them back. A safety copy of your current profiles is made first.
- **Test run** — shows exactly what would happen without changing anything.
- Nothing is ever deleted. No internet connection, no tracking, nothing uploaded.
